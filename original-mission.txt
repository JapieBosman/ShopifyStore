# MISSION: Architecture & Schema Design for "Shopify TradePOS & B2B Debtors"

### Context & Background:
We are an established retail ERP software company (Genesis Retail Solutions) with 25+ years of battle-tested domain depth in brick-and-mortar retail, trade counters, point-of-sale registers, and accounts receivable.
We are now entering the Shopify ecosystem to build a standalone, high-growth B2B SaaS application. 

### Market Opportunity & The Problem:
* **The Scale:** Over 270,000 brick-and-mortar stores use Shopify POS, processing over $100 Billion in offline in-store sales (growing at 32% YoY).
* **The Fatal Blind Spot in Shopify:** Shopify was architected for consumer eCommerce with immediate card checkout. When trade businesses (hardware stores, building materials, electrical & plumbing supply, auto spares, agricultural supply) use Shopify in-store, they hit a brick wall:
  1. **No "Charge to Account" at POS:** Cashiers cannot let trade contractors charge to a 30-day account.
  2. **No Debtors / AR Engine:** Shopify has no concept of credit limits, 30/60/90/120-day aged balance calculations, payment allocations, or monthly PDF statement runs.
  3. **No Web/PC Till:** Shopify POS only runs on iPads and Android tablets. Traditional trade counters with Windows PCs, barcode scanners, and thermal receipt printers have no browser-based till.
  4. **Shopify's Alternative is Flawed:** Shopify gates basic B2B behind Shopify Plus ($2,500/month), and even then, it only works for online self-service ordering, completely failing at the physical counter till. Existing third-party apps either act as factoring fintechs taking 3-5% transaction cuts or are basic gift-card credit tools lacking real accounting depth.

### Our Assets & Domain Rules (From Genesis):
We possess proven domain logic and mathematical rules harvested from our core modules (`genDEB`, `genTIL`, `genCOF`), including:
1. **Debtor Aging Engine:** 8-bucket aging calculation (Current, 30, 60, 90, 120, 150, 180, Over). Credit allocation is applied oldest-debt-first (`Over -> 180 -> ... -> Current`).
2. **Statement Generation:** Opening balance calculation, newest-first invoice reconciliation, generation of itemized monthly statements with remittance advice, and finance interest calculation on 60+ day overdue accounts.
3. **Counter Till Controls:** Credit limit enforcement, credit hold alerts, supervisor PIN overrides for over-limit sales, mandatory capture of Customer Purchase Order (PO) Number and Job Reference on slips, and tender-integrity refunds.
4. **Cash Office & Register Shifts:** Opening floats, shift cash drops (with reason codes), blind denomination counting (notes and coins), and automated shortage/surplus reconciliation.
5. **Pricing Grids:** Trade price codes (Retail vs Wholesale vs Trade), volume breaks (quantity tiers), and contractor-specific price lists.

### Architectural Constraint (CRITICAL):
**DO NOT replicate the legacy Genesis Delphi/SQL Server database schema.** 
The old Genesis database is a legacy schema (monolithic wide tables, cryptic abbreviations like `STKMaster`, `DEBMaster`, `TxTp`, string-based tenant scoping `gCO`/`gBranch`, and database triggers).
**The new app must be engineered with modern SaaS standards:**
* **Backend:** Python (Django REST Framework) or Node.js/TypeScript.
* **Frontend:** Next.js (React) using Shopify Polaris design system for Admin, and Shopify POS UI Extensions / modern Web Till for cashiers.
* **Database:** Multi-tenant PostgreSQL, cleanly normalized (3NF), indexed, and referencing Shopify Global IDs (`gid://shopify/...`).
* **Deployment:** Fully containerized via Docker (e.g. deployed on AWS, Render, or Google Cloud Run).
